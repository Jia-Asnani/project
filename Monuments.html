Monuments

The city has one of the most monuments in the world.

L’ arc triomphe

l’ arc triomphe is a memorial for the soldiers lost lives in world war 1 and the French Revolution, that significantly impacted France. l’arc de triomphe consists of a fire to remember the lost lives of soldiers. Soldiers names are encraved in the walls of the triomphe.

L’ Eiffel Tower

Eiffel Tower was bully in 1889 by Gustave Eiffel. It was a structural masterpiece of 1800s engineering. It is made completely from iron metal. Going on top of the Eiffel tower is made accessible with stairs. It is made accessible for old people, children and disable individuals with elevators, continuously going up and down the tower. There are restaurants on the first floor with seating up to 500 people, making food accessible to large amount of people inside the Eiffel Tower. Large amounts of people visit during the night to the Tower. The Eiffel tower lights up with more than 20000 blinking lights for 5 minutes every hour. But, entering the tower is not allowed at night.

Olympic Cauldron

From mid summer holidays to mid September, the “big Helium balloon” is put up. Every evening in the sunset, the balloon is lit up and rises 60 meters up into the distance. The balloon makes a dremy background for perfect photos.

Seine River

Seine river is one of the oldest rivers in Europe. The river runs through the center of the Paris city. Cruises range from 14 euros to the overnight cruise to over 100 euros. Five star restaurants in the cruise serve food and drinks throughout the night. Boating in the Seine river takes tourists throughout the city and the sightseeing of the most famous Eiffel Tower, Le Louvre museum and Notre Dame church in Paris.
