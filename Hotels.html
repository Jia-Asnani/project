Hotels

Le Louis Boutique

Its low budget but interior is designed with Jazz history. It is 115 euros per night with a classy feel perfect for student budgets. The hotel has a rooftop that offers a the picturesque street view of Paris.

Hotel Des Balcons

The hotel is located besides the Boulevard St Michael and luxemborg gardens with direct view from the road sided bedrooms. France is famous for the French balcony architecture. This hotel has the small French balconies in 30 of 45 rooms with the view of the street. It is a 2 star low budget restaurant of 145 euros per night with the authenic French architecture.

Hotel Massé

This hotel costs more than the Hotel Balcons with a budget of 220 per room. But, the hotel is located at a walking distance from Gare du Noord in Paris saving up on the transport costs. The hotel has a nice view of the street from the rooms. Additionally, there is art exhibitions occasionally organized in the lobby of the hotel that can be viewed and bought.
