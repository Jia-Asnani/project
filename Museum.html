Museums

The city has the famous museums for art, history and fashion.

Le Louvre

Famous for its historical portrayal of the Mona Lisa in the museum Le Louvre. The museum is built underground and has more than 30,000 artworks. The works in the museum include famous portraits and paintings. It is important to arrive early in the morning at the museum to avoid the crowds. It tends to get busy in the evenings.

Musée D'Orsay

Museum for contemporary art is this museum. Sculptures, paintings, furnitures and photography are all present in this museum. If you are interested in art around the world and want to explore contemporary art works, this museum is a must visit.

Le Galeries Dior

France has a major contribution to the fashion industry. Brands like Chanel and Dior originated in France. Le galerie dior is a museum portraying the advances in fashion in Dior since 100 years.It shows most of the famous historic and recent Dior fashion trends. The museum is a great representation of the elegance of french fashion.
