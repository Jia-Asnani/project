Visit Paris

The City of Lights

Paris is a city with amazing European mix of culture, romance, glamour and fine cuisine. This city is known worldwide for its rich culture and history. World famous Churches, Palaces and Museums with large architectural museums to visit. Paris, famous for its magnificent architecture. World famous Eiffel Tower takes you in Paris with a view of the whole city from the top.

Monuments portray the world renowned French fashion and the historical churches. Cruising on the Seine river and strolling in the streets offer a great stroll through the city.

Paris is also called the “Capital of Gastronomy” for its prestigious cuisine. The cuisine is known for its delicacy and soft flavours making it world famous. Restaurants from fine dining to traditional food to a freshly baked French croissant are easily accessible in the streets. The city is home for world renowned fashion houses of Louis Vuitton and Chanel that are symbols for elegance and luxury in the fashion industry. Paris gives numerous shopping aids for the fashion loving tourists.
