Visit Paris

The City of Lights

Paris is a must visit when visiting Europe as a mix of culture, romance and fantastic restaurants! Paris is a city known worldwide for its rich culture and history. World famous Churches, Palaces and Museums with large architectural museums to visit. Paris is famous for its magnificent architecture. The world famous Eiffel Tower takes you in Paris with a view of the whole city from the top.

Museums portray the world renowned French fashion and the historical artworks. Cruises run in the day and night in the Seine river for a joyful ride through the city.

Paris is also called the “Capital of Gastronomy” for its prestigious cuisine. The cuisine is known for its delicacy and soft flavours making it world famous. Restaurants from fine dining to traditional food to a freshly baked French croissant are easily accessible in the streets at affordable prices for students. Many affordable hotels are located in the centre of the city, listed further in this website. The city is home for world renowned fashion houses of Louis Vuitton and Chanel that are symbols for elegance and luxury in the fashion industry. Paris gives numerous shopping sites for the fashion loving tourists.
